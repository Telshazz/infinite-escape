## Main description (sell page body)

### Every group gets a different room

Tell Infinite Escape who's playing and it builds the game around you.
A family with kids gets a gentle guide who never lets them stay stuck.
A team of veteran puzzle-hunters gets decoys, codes hidden two rooms away,
a Game Master who stays silent, and exactly one hint per puzzle — spend it
wisely. Choose your world, your intensity, and your hour. Nothing on the
setup screen is cosmetic: every answer changes what gets built.

### An AI Game Master runs your session

It narrates your progress, tracks every objective, fires the room's
physical effects on story beats, and answers when you call for help —
subtle at first, clearer each time you ask. On easy settings it watches
over your shoulder and steps in before frustration does. On expert, it
watches in silence.

### Puzzles you touch, not menus you click

Walk up to the altar. Look closely at the compass. Wire the breaker panel
with your own hands. Every puzzle lives on a real object in the room, and
every world plays differently — sacred sequences in Atlantis, bearing locks
on the pirate deck, circuit reroutes in the quarantine lab, riddles from a
stone leviathan.

### Seven worlds. One build. Zero construction.

The physical space never changes — the same three chambers, the same
hardware. The software swaps everything else in seconds: story, puzzles,
lighting, soundscape, even the shape of the route (Wonderland famously
refuses to be linear). When your group escapes the temple, the next group
can board the galleon — no crew, no drywall, no downtime.

### Feel the room respond

Fans become ocean currents. Misters become thermal vents. Light channels
pulse when seals ignite, and sound moves around you as you walk. Infinite
Escape choreographs real, physical effects hardware to the beats of your
story — at an intensity tuned to exactly how scared you asked to be.

## For venue operators (B2B strip)

**Stop rebuilding. Start regenerating.** A traditional escape room theme
costs $80K–$200K to build and is stale in two years. Infinite Escape turns
one capital build — three chambers and an effects rig — into an unlimited
theme library with software margins. New inventory is a menu choice, not a
construction project. Repeat customers aren't a hope; they're the design.

## For investors (one-liner)

The fixed layer is the venue's capex. The swappable layer — story, puzzles,
pacing, atmosphere — is recurring software. That split is the moat, and the
demo lets you walk through it in first person.
